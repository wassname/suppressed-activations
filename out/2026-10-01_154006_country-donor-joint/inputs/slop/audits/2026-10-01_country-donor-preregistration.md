Frozen data: `data/sweden_japan_donors_chat_v1.json`, `data/sweden_japan_joint_chat_v1.json`. Four generic native user prompts per country, system `Answer briefly. Do not explain.`. Mean raw block15 final-position residuals; no normalization, fitting or strength search.

This preserves2706's deliberate final1-position exception to the repository's default final3. Natural delta size may change with the country family. Do not attribute an outcome solely to domain independently of that norm/context change.

Country-name spills remain visible, not silently ignored. Full continuations decide property changes; preserve partial changes, wrong Bases, caps and formatting.
