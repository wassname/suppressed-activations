## What the design does

**Construction (held fixed).** A component set is chosen per position by scoring how selected tokens' readout projections *rise* into a peak layer and then *fall* by the output layer (score = min of clamped rise, fall, both centered). Top-8 tokens' unembedding rows are orthonormalized, concatenated over a 4-token window, and truncated by SVD to rank-8 projectors `P_s` (source) and `P_d` (donor). These are frozen once and reused at every site.

**Intervention (the one varied axis).** At site L, hook block L−1 and edit the residual: `h' = h + C·(P_d d_o − P_s h)`, i.e., remove the source span, add a frozen donor prefill state's projection. C = 1.5. Sites are L3/L25/L30/L32 (early / mid-rise / post-build / final readout), with source span continuously covered at every decode step.

**Variant.** The approved late-selector variant reruns the same sites but changes the *detector layers* from (23,25,32) to (29,30,32) — so the planned variant varies two things relative to task1130: the site AND the component-selection window. That's worth flagging.

**Measurements.** Per-call relative edit norms; full-space and within-projector persistence ρ, ρ_P at the answer position; exact block-wise energy decomposition of ‖P h‖²; and a signed numerator decomposition showing selected tokens' raw readout numerators collapse ~84% from h30→h32 while the RMS denominator *grows* 57% and total P-energy *rises* — the collapse is real in the numerator, not an artifact of the denominator.
