## 2. Objection to the random control (DeepSeek's point, deserves emphasis)

`random_delta = randn_like(Δ)` lives in the **full** space, not the U-span. So `C(random_delta - span)` adds an orthogonal component of norm ~C‖Δ‖ that the real delta never has. The RAND cells are not matched interventions; they are matched-norm + extra off-span disruption. This cuts both ways:

- legs-dog-RAND's digit→4 could be an off-span artifact, weakening Kimi's I3 (digit = easy low-dim readout);
- but it also means RAND "conservative disruption" framing in the earlier naming base is muddled.

**Fix:** project the random delta into the span (`random_delta = U(Uᵀ randn)`, norm-matched) so the only difference from the real condition is direction *within* the span. That's the control the design intends and doesn't have.
