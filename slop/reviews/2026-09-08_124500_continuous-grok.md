# Skeptical review: continuous concept replacement

## Strongest misconception
The method treats **mean Jacobian rows of a three-word final-layer energy** as an editable spider/dog identity plane. Those rows are linear sensitivities of later `unembed(word)·gain` mass to earlier residuals on generic WikiText. They are not proven carriers of the implicit “web-spinner → spider” fact, nor a manifold where coordinate exchange yields a new animal identity. Digit top-1 under C1/C2 only shows the readout tilted; full strings already falsify concept replacement (riddles, retracts, beetle, return to eight). **First-digit change ≠ success**—you already have that negative result.

## What the estimator actually validates
FD vs autograd and split-half cosine only support “this gradient is stable numerics.” They do not support semantics, suppressed-span causality, or post-edit source readout. Three lexical rows plus WikiText-averaged J cannot isolate the prompt-specific association. Projecting into a rank-4 “suppressed” span built from rise/fall of arbitrary top-8 vocab rows is under-motivated; your own animal-name scan found almost nothing. Gate stops instant re-swap of the same state; it does not stop new tokens from recreating source dominance or off-manifold drift (C2 as threefold extrapolation along d).

## Donor margin (proposed)
`minimum_norm_change_to_reach(measured_clean_donor_contrast)` is a better **dose** than blind C∈{1,2,4}, but still the **same axis family** (word-J pair). It likely buys a softer lexical/logit bias toward “dog”/“ant” and a matched legs digit—not a preserved semantic state. Expect fewer cartoon failures at matched margin, not reliable dog/ant biology under paraphrase. Treat as ablation of overshoot, not the concept fix.

## Concrete alternative
Drop word-J planes as the edit basis. Build a **prompt-contrast steering vector** from clean forwards only:
- Source template: web-spinner legs fact (and close paraphrases).
- Donor templates: same frame with dog/ant filled (“legs on a dog/ant”).
- Δ(layer) = mean residual difference on the answer-bearing span (and optionally the animal NP), not vocab Jacobians.
- Single minimum-norm apply on last prefill positions (and at most first decode) to match donor margin on the legs digit **and** on a held-out attribute probe; no per-token fight unless a continuity check fails.
- Optional: ablate web→spider attention/value path if localized, rather than global residual swap.

This targets the factual association geometry instead of “make dog-row energy exchange with spider-row energy.”

## Cheapest matched discriminator
Do **not** score success by legs digit. After the same edit, run 3–5 frozen probes that never mention legs/count:
- “This animal’s covering is mainly…” / “It moves by…” / “It spins webs: yes/no”
- Forced choice: bark vs mandibles vs spinnerets  
True replacement → donor-consistent attributes and low spinneret/web affirmation. Digit-only / lexical bias → spider attributes remain, web=yes, or incoherence. Same corpus strength, same layers; compare edit vs clean donor forward. Cost: few short gens, no new selector.

## Ranked hypotheses and falsifiers
1. **J-word plane is mostly lexical readout tilt** (best fit to current fails).  
   *Pred:* margin-matched edit flips digit often; attribute probes stay spider; paraphrases (“silk producer”) restore 8.
2. **Global every-token swap fights dynamics; identity never settles.**  
   *Pred:* last-3-prefill-only Δ(prompt-contrast) beats per-token J-swap on coherence at same digit rate; traces show less delta thrash.
3. **Suppressed rank-4 span is unrelated to this fact.**  
   *Pred:* random rank-4 same-energy projection matches “retain 8” at L24; animal names stay absent from selector tops.
4. **True concept replace needs association-site edit, not residual pair flip.**  
   *Pred:* prompt-contrast Δ or web-token path ablation shifts attributes without riddles; J-swap never does at equal residual norm.

If (1) and (3) hold, further C-grid on this pair is low value.
