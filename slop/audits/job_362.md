At $C\geq32$, the other continuations became unrelated or malformed. Removing either normalization did not reveal a coherent spider → 4 transition.

## Metric audit

`swap_log_odds_shift` reached +5.50 nats, but it only compares the probabilities of bare first tokens `4` and `8`. Formatting tokens such as `**` or `The` can become the argmax while the later text still says 8. `p(4)+p(8)` is therefore bare-answer mass, not general coherence. Repeated-bigram fraction detects loops only.
