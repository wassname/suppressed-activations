## Control-magnitude check (candidate vs random; same C is NOT magnitude matching)

Random total-applied-norm is consistently ~1.7-2.4x the candidate (e.g. prop-P1-spinneret-dog
cand 804 vs rand 1966). The random control is the floor but is not magnitude-matched; the
candidate's 6-vs-1 primary is therefore a direction-specific signal that may be understated (a
larger random perturbation moving less semantically is evidence FOR the candidate), but the
control is imperfect and labelled as such. A magnitude-matched rerun is the separating test.
