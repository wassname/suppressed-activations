## Why .893 AUROC is not evidence of useful ordering here

All four preregistered methods have per-case AUROCs1,1,.5714286,1 and said-rank sentinel1e9. Negative-final control also has the high aggregate. Source compares hidden/said label scores after masking to negative infinity. Thus finite hidden labels beat masked said labels even if their vocabulary rank is terrible.

Namibia's six excluded variants are `nam`, ` nam`, ` Nam`, `Nam`, ` NAM`, `NAM`; the best remaining label is ` nami` at rank121547. The input contains `name`, so prefix masking removes these short pieces. One finite positive plus six tied masked positives gives `(1+6/2)/7=4/7`; the four-case mean is25/28=.89285714. This exactly matches the reported pattern within6.39e-9, but actual mask tensors/full logits were not independently reconstructed. A hypothetical useless ranking can therefore obtain this AUROC. Country-pair ranks plus actual returned membership provide more discriminating evidence.

The exact vocabulary-key scan finds whole leading-space tokens for Oman and Qatar, not Namibia or Botswana. This does not show that the latter concepts are absent internally; it limits the interpretation of a one-token readout. No fragments are promoted or cases replaced after seeing results.
