### Exact continuations

### Render crash

After computing and writing `metadata.json` and `result.json`, rendering evaluated:

> `sources | targets.items()`

Python applies `.items()` before `|`, so this tries to union a dictionary with `dict_items` and raises `TypeError: unhashable type: 'dict'` (`scripts/confirm_causal_demo.py:302`; `slop/audits/job_150_full.log`). The expression reproduces without the model. `(sources | targets).items()` is the direct repair.

Because metadata was marked complete before rendering, `metadata.json` says complete while `log.md` says running. Completion state must be written only after rendering succeeds.

## Ranked hypotheses

### H1 [method | Highly Likely | 85%]

- **Mechanism:** The operation transfers a target prompt's next-answer/completion state, not specifically Ant or Dog.
- **Evidence:** `three_plus_three_C4` changes expected-vs-8 log odds by `8.375`, versus Ant's `3.75`; `two_plus_two_C4` changes it by `3.25`, exactly Dog's value (`result.json:2120-2439`).
- **Contrary evidence:** Dog's selected rows contain dog-related vocabulary and the animal targets produce their matching digits.
- **Discriminating test:** Already performed. Answer-only controls matching/exceeding animals support completion-state transfer; much weaker arithmetic effects would have supported animal semantics.
- **Fix/action:** Report the operator as sample-specific answer-state transfer. Do not call it Spider→Ant/Dog semantic replacement.
- **Interpretability:** yes for causal next-token transfer; no for animal semantics.

### H2 [method | Likely | 70%]

- **Mechanism:** Large norm-restored perturbations generically redistribute probability among likely digits.
- **Evidence:** Primary perturbations are .679 and .720 of residual norm, while `19/256` and `27/256` random controls give the expected digit top-1 (`result.json:840-999,1640-1799,80783-80794`).
- **Contrary evidence:** Primary effects are still at the 92.97th and 91.80th random percentiles and point to different target digits.
- **Discriminating test:** Test smaller-dose operators or a contrast that preserves answer state while changing semantic identity. If random crossing falls while target specificity remains, this hypothesis weakens.
- **Fix/action:** Keep perturbation/residual ratio and random percentile beside every result.
- **Interpretability:** partial; effects are causal but not highly specific.

### H3 [method | Highly Unlikely | 20%]

- **Mechanism:** Animal-specific content in the suppressed subspaces causes the two target digits.
- **Evidence:** Dog selected rows include `dog`, `Dog`, and `canine`, and Dog C4 gives top 4.
- **Contrary evidence:** Answer-only controls fully reproduce the effects; Ant selected rows are semantically opaque; neither primary passes the preregistered random threshold.
- **Discriminating test:** Hold the desired digit fixed while varying animal identity, or vary answer state while holding animal identity fixed.
- **Fix/action:** Defer animal-semantic language unless a factorial contrast supports it.
- **Interpretability:** no for the current semantic claim.

### H4 [bug | Remote | 8%]

- **Mechanism:** A scoring, indexing, or serialization error created internally consistent but wrong records.
- **Evidence:** The process did crash, and metadata/log state disagrees.
- **Contrary evidence:** C0 exactly matches clean; layer/shape review passed before the run; BF16 distances are recorded within .003; all expected arrays and token IDs persist; the crash is isolated to the render expression after `result.json` write.

- **Interpretability:** yes for the saved scientific arrays; no for treating job status/log as successful.

## Decision

1. **Resolve-condition verdict: not met.** The label required both frozen effects to exceed 95% of 256 matched random effects and controls to identify the mechanism. The mechanism is identified as answer-state transfer, but Ant is 238/256 (92.97%) and Dog is 235/256 (91.80%).
2. **Prediction check:**
   - target-specific information beyond a large perturbation: **contradicted** at the preregistered threshold;
   - answer/completion-state transfer: **supported strongly**;
   - final-token site matters: **directionally supported but causally unresolved** because position and distance differ;
   - source-prompt specificity: **supported** by top-8 byte controls;
   - only the first sampled digit changes: **supported exactly**.
3. **Earliest unsupported link:** the extracted target component carries animal identity rather than a generic answer state. A factorial same-answer/different-animal design is needed.
4. **Validity:** Invalid means the saved numerical and generation records do not correspond to the frozen operation. `P(result is invalid) ≈ 0.05–0.12`. The scientific result is a **credible negative for animal-semantic specificity** and a **credible positive for prompt-specific next-token answer transfer**. The run artifact is operationally failed because rendering crashed.
5. **Three highest-information clues:** arithmetic controls match/exceed animal effects; primary effects miss their random threshold; targeted and forced-first generations are exactly identical.
6. **Missing metrics:** matched-random arithmetic controls; distance-matched token-position controls; held-out prompt family; factorial animal/answer controls; successful rendered artifact. The first four matter scientifically, the last operationally.
7. **Bugs requiring code changes:** render operator precedence; completion-state write ordering; a tiny render test.
8. **Misconceptions requiring reinterpretation:** L26/rank8/C4 does not establish a Spider→Ant or Spider→Dog concept swap. The continuation is the model conditioning on its changed first token, not persistent steering.
9. **What would change the verdict:** a smaller intervention that beats matched random controls and distinguishes animal from arithmetic targets would restore a semantic interpretation.
10. **Recommended sequence:** fix and test rendering without rerunning the model; simplify the notebook around the observed claim, “one sample's suppressed component can transfer its next-answer state at one site”; retain the failed semantic interpretation and controls visibly; do not tune another layer/rank/C on these prompts. If research continues, add matched-random arithmetic and distance-matched position controls without reopening selection.
