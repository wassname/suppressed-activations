### H1 [method | Highly Likely | 75%]

- **Mechanism:** The projected LM-head coordinate operation writes token-aligned lexical content but does not replace the latent animal concept needed for the answer.
- **Evidence:** At restored mean C=2, the log reports `top= Spider` while `p6=1.3572015689078398e-07`. The continuation then says “the animal that spins webs is a **spider**.”
- **Contrary evidence:** Mean C=1.5 sometimes inserts a `6` hypothesis, and mild doses raise p6. Ant-related influence is therefore plausible.
- **Discriminating test:** Log the Spider and Ant coordinates/readout before and after each patch. A valid swap should lower Spider and raise Ant before answer logits change; lexical source amplification predicts Spider rises or Ant does not.
- **Fix/action:** Add explicit direction checks to the existing script before any more broad sweeps.
- **Interpretability:** `yes` for the observed negative result; `no` for calling the operation a successful Ant concept insertion.

4. **Validity:** Invalid means that the stored rows do not correspond to the declared input, code, or intervention, or cannot answer the preregistered grid question. `P(result is invalid)≈0.10–0.20`. Classification: **credible negative for this prompt, method set, positions, layers, and dose grid**; not a general negative about suppressed-subspace intervention.
5. **Three highest-information clues:** (1) all six C=2 rows switch directly to `Spider`; this locates a shared failure transition. (2) restored mean C=2 has positive 6-vs-8 odds only because both are near zero; this prevents a false positive. (3) C=1.5 text writes then rejects the 6 hypothesis; this shows lexical influence without the desired answer change.
6. **Missing metrics:** (1) pre/post Spider and Ant coordinates; (2) localized-position and layer curves; (3) output KL and perturbation/residual ratio; (4) matched-random effects at mild C; (5) repeat prompts/seeds.
7. **Bugs requiring code changes:** H4 → synthetic operator test and model-coordinate assertions. The proposed RMSNorm gain bug was checked and rejected.
8. **Misconceptions requiring reinterpretation:** C>1 is extrapolation past a full coordinate exchange. Positive 6-vs-8 odds are meaningless when another token dominates. Fluency after the first token does not establish semantic replacement.
9. **What would change the verdict:** A localized row with top `6`, noncollapsed p6, ordinary output entropy, lower Spider/higher Ant coordinate, coherent 64-token text, and a larger effect than matched random would turn this into a positive causal example.
10. **Recommended sequence:** First add coordinate, KL, and perturbation logging. Then run the localized dense transition. Only generate text for valid top-6 candidates. Keep the README's direct Spider→Ant result negative unless such a candidate survives matched controls.
