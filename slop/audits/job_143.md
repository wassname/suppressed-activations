## Hypotheses

### H1 [method | Highly Likely | 80%]

- Mechanism: C=8–11.6 extrapolations move the residual by 23.1858, 91% of its norm, causing generic digit redistribution rather than a semantic animal exchange.
- Evidence: `log.md` records C values 8.0000, 11.0575, and 11.6056 at distance 23.1858; target answers are 6, 6, and 4/6 rather than 6, 4, and 2.
- Contrary evidence: expected-token probability rises for all three targets relative to clean, so some target-specific component may exist.
- Discriminating test: run C=1. Target-specific diagonal probability gains support semantics; no pattern supports extrapolation artifact.
- Fix/action: measure the C=1 probability matrix before further large-dose runs.
- Interpretability: yes as a negative result for this large-extrapolation implementation; no as evidence of semantic replacement.

### H2 [measurement | Likely | 75%]

- Mechanism: the spider prompt’s output distribution is easy to move among common digit tokens under a large residual perturbation.
- Evidence: matched random controls yield top 6 five times, top 4 three times, and top 2 twice; random 6-vs-8 odds reach 4.25, stronger than the targeted 2.375.
- Contrary evidence: byte remains stable under its own smaller prompt-specific edit.
- Discriminating test: compare animal targets against random vocabulary targets passed through the same projection-and-coordinate operation at C=1 and equal distance.
- Fix/action: use structured random target-token controls after the C=1 diagnostic.
- Interpretability: yes; the current null establishes substantial generic digit instability at the chosen distance.

### H3 [method | Highly Unlikely | 25%]

- Mechanism: projected animal directions contain weak semantics, but C and the basis do not isolate them enough for top-1 mapping.
- Evidence: p(expected) rises from clean for Ant (.0267→.6147), Dog p(4) (.0564→.1267), and Bird p(2) (.0067→.1127).
- Contrary evidence: the wrong digit dominates Dog and Bird; random ranks miss the preregistered criterion; post-readouts contain no target animal.

- Fix/action: retain probabilities rather than requiring only top-1.
- Interpretability: partial; current data motivate but do not establish this hypothesis.

### H4 [bug | Almost Certain | 99%]

2. Prediction check: target-animal semantics **contradicted** at the preregistered criterion; prompt-specific digit perturbation **supported**; calibration/coordinate bug **contradicted** because distances and norms match.
3. Earliest unsupported link: projected source/target LM-head coordinates have not been shown to encode an animal identity exchange. A target-specific C=1 digit-probability matrix would support it.
4. Validity: invalid means code/provenance failure that prevents interpreting the measured conditions. `P(result is invalid) ≈ 0.08`; classification: **credible negative for the tested large-extrapolation semantic mapping**, not a negative for the general idea.
5. Highest-information clues: (1) failed three-way mapping, because it directly tests specificity; (2) equal distances, because it rules out simple dose mismatch; (3) broad random digit changes, because it supplies scale.
6. Missing metrics: C=1 target-by-digit matrix; structured random-token targets; target-animal readout under a fixed basis; same-direction held-out prompt behavior.
